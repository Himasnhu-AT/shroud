TELLS

A first-person shooter where the enemy squad learns from how you play.

The match
  Bomb defusal, up to 5 rounds, first to 3 wins. You attack alone: plant
  the bomb at site A or B (stand in the painted site, hold E). The squad
  defends. You win a round if the bomb explodes or the whole squad is down.
  You lose it if you die before planting, the clock runs out with no plant,
  or they defuse.
  Squad size depends on difficulty: Recruit 3, Regular 4, Veteran 5,
  Elite 6. New players start on Recruit; change it on the Play screen.

Money
  You start with $800. Each round pays: $3,250 for a win, $1,400 for a loss
  (more for each loss in a row, up to $3,400), plus kill rewards and $300 for
  a plant. Press B to buy guns, armor and grenades at the start of a round,
  near your spawn. Survive a round and you keep your gear.

Controls
  WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, Ctrl or C crouch, mouse look
  Left mouse shoot, Right mouse aim, R reload
  1 / 2 or mouse wheel switch guns, Q last weapon, V inspect
  E (hold) plant the bomb in a site, or pick up a gun
  4 or G equip / cycle grenades, then Left mouse throw overhand,
    Right mouse underhand
  B buy menu, Tab (hold) scoreboard, Esc pause / release the mouse
  F3 see what the enemies are planning

How the squad learns
  After every match the squad evolves against a model of how YOU play:
  where you hold, which way you peek, the ranges you fight at. Their
  reaction time, aim and damage are fixed by the difficulty you pick and
  never change: they get smarter, never faster. The debrief screen leads
  with what the squad learned about you.

Your progress and the AI's memory live in
  __DATA_DIR__
  (delete that folder to start over)

Crash and performance reports
  The game sends crash reports, frame times and match results (map,
  difficulty, round scores) to the developer, with no name attached. What
  the squad learns about you is never sent. Turn it off in Settings >
  Gameplay, or set DO_NOT_TRACK=1.

Feedback welcome on the itch.io page: what felt unfair, what felt smart,
what broke.
