SHROUD - review build

Install: drag Shroud into Applications, then open it.

Controls
  WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, Ctrl or C crouch
  Mouse look, Left click shoot, R reload, Esc release the mouse
  F2 performance stats, F3 see what the enemies are planning

How it works
  You fight a squad of 5 in best-of-3 rounds. After every match the squad
  evolves against a model of how YOU play: where you hold, which way you
  peek, your range, your reactions. Each match should get harder.
  The debrief screen shows what they learned about you.

Your progress and the AI's memory live in
  ~/Library/Application Support/shroud   (delete that folder to start over)

Feedback welcome: what felt unfair, what felt smart, what broke.
