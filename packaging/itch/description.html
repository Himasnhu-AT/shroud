A tactical shooter where the enemy squad reads your tells, and plays against them.

Bomb defusal, you against a squad of three to six (by difficulty), first to 3 rounds: plant at A or B before the clock runs out. After every match the squad evolves against a model of you: where you hold, which way you peek, the ranges you fight at. The next squad is bred from the ones that did best against you, and it plays differently.

What's in this build (v0.2.0)

  • An evolving squad: traits (aggression, flanking, cover use, range) evolve with a genetic algorithm, combat moves (strafing, peeking, when to shoot) with NEAT neural nets, and tactics adapt to where they predict you'll be.
  • Fair by design: reaction time, aim error and damage are fixed by difficulty and never change. They get smarter, never faster.
  • Squad tactics: roles (rifleman, rusher, breacher, marksman), callouts, flanks, frag and flash grenades, and lights shot out over a player who camps.
  • A round economy: money, freeze time and a buy menu with 8 guns (pistols, SMG, shotgun, rifles, scout and sniper), armor and grenades. Survive and you keep your gear. The squad runs its own economy.
  • 6 maps: Sirocco, Gantry, Township, Depot, Ledger and Foundry.
  • Debrief: after each match it leads with what the squad learned about you, what they tried, whether it worked and what they'll do next time, then how the squad changed.
  • Bodycam mode (Settings → Video): wide-lens chest-cam look, off by default.

Controls

  • WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, Ctrl / C crouch, Mouse look
  • Left click shoot, Right click aim, R reload, 1 / 2 or wheel switch guns, Q last weapon
  • E (hold) plant the bomb in site A or B, or pick up a gun
  • 4 / G equip and cycle grenades, then Left click overhand, Right click underhand
  • B buy menu (start of round), Tab scoreboard, Esc pause / release the mouse, F3 see what the enemies are planning

Install

  • Windows 10/11 (64-bit): unzip and run Tells.exe. No installer, no extra runtimes.
  • macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel): unzip and open Tells.app. Signed and notarized.
  • The itch app installs and launches either one.

Your progress and the squad's memory are saved locally; delete the save folder (see README.txt) to start over.

Crash and performance reports

This build sends crash reports, frame times and match results (map, difficulty, round scores) so we can fix what breaks, with no name attached. What the squad learns about you never leaves your computer. Turn it off in Settings → Gameplay.
